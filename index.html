How pressure builds through an innings

Pressure is how much one ball can swing the result. It rises through chases and peaks in the last overs. The dashed line is the average ball. In the first innings it is highest in the powerplay, where an early wicket costs most.

Does leverage beat simpler pressure rules?

Each rule flags the same share of balls (about 19%). Persistence is how well a player's pressure rating in one half of the matches matches the other half; the random row is the noise floor.
